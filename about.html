About Cortis :

CORTIS is a new generation boygroup that debuted with a concept of boundless energy and freedom. Formed by five extraordinary talents with different backgrounds, they are united by a vision to create music that speaks the truth and inspires the younger generation.

Blending Pop, R&B, and modern EDM elements, CORTIS is known for their addictive melodies, powerful vocals, and sharp, captivating choreography. They continue to prove themselves as future icons in the global music industry, bringing an authentic charm to every masterpiece they create.

Members Profile

JAMES

Leader / Main Vocal

James is the calm yet charismatic leader of the group, boasting extraordinary vocal abilities and a nurturing leadership style that protects all members.

JUHOON

Main Dancer / Visual

Known for his precise dance style and sharp, intense gaze on stage, Juhoon is the main visual who always commands attention.

KEONHO

Keonho is characterized by his deep rap voice and explosive stage energy. He also frequently produces songs for CORTIS.

MARTIN

SEONGHYEON
